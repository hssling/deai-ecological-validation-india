# PROSPERO Registration Draft

## Review Title

Community-deliverable interventions for physical frailty in older adults: a systematic review, component network meta-analysis, and implementation-readiness mapping for Indian primary care.

## Review Question

Among prefrail or frail older adults, which community-deliverable non-pharmacological interventions improve frailty and physical function, and which intervention components are most feasible for implementation in Indian/LMIC primary care?

## Search Sources

PubMed/MEDLINE, Embase if accessible, Cochrane CENTRAL, Europe PMC, Crossref/OpenAlex, ClinicalTrials.gov, WHO ICTRP, and citation chasing of key reviews and included trials.

## Population

Adults aged 60 years and older, or studies with mean age 65 years and older, identified as prefrail, frail, sarcopenic/frail or functionally vulnerable.

## Intervention

Exercise, nutrition, exercise plus nutrition, multidomain care, home-based or digital physical-activity support, caregiver-supported interventions and related non-pharmacological frailty interventions.

## Comparator

Usual care, waitlist, education, attention control, placebo supplement or active comparator.

## Outcomes

Frailty status, frailty score, gait speed, SPPB, grip strength, ADL/IADL, falls, quality of life, adherence, dropout and adverse events.

## Risk of Bias

Cochrane RoB 2.

## Data Synthesis

Random-effects pairwise meta-analysis. Component network meta-analysis will be conditional on a connected and clinically coherent network. GRADE/CINeMA-style certainty assessment will be used where feasible.

## Planned Review Status

Preliminary scoping has been undertaken to refine eligibility and feasibility. No final study selection, extraction or synthesis has been completed at the time of this draft.

## Planned Subgroups

- community/home/primary-care versus residential/nursing-home setting
- exercise only versus nutrition only versus exercise plus nutrition versus multidomain intervention
- supervised versus self-directed or digitally supported delivery
- frailty-specific trials versus sarcopenia-only trials with functional outcomes
- shorter interventions (<12 weeks) versus longer interventions (>=12 weeks)
