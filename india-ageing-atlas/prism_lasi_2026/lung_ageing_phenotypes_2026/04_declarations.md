# Declarations

**Manuscript:** A Metabolic-Inflammatory Reserve Axis Underlies Lung-Function Variation in Middle-Aged and Older Indians: A Data-Driven Analysis of the Longitudinal Ageing Study in India.

**Author:** Dr Siddalingaiah H S, MD, Professor, Department of Community Medicine, Shridevi Institute of Medical Sciences and Research Hospital, Tumkur, Karnataka, India.

## Ethics approval and consent to participate

Secondary analysis of de-identified, publicly available LASI Wave 1 data. LASI obtained ethical approval from the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) and written informed consent from all participants. No additional approval was required.

## Consent for publication

Not applicable; no individually identifiable data.

## Availability of data and material

LASI Wave 1 data are available through the Gateway to Global Aging Data (g2aging.org) and IIPS. The clustering and analysis code are available from the author on reasonable request.
