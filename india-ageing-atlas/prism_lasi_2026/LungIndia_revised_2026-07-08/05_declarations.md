# Declarations

**Manuscript:** How Much Preserved Ratio Impaired Spirometry in Older Indians Is Real? National Prevalence, Metabolic and Ageing Correlates, and Dependence on the Reference Equation in the Longitudinal Ageing Study in India.

**Author:** Dr Siddalingaiah H S, MD, Professor, Department of Community Medicine, Shridevi Institute of Medical Sciences and Research Hospital, Tumkur, Karnataka, India.

## Ethics approval and consent to participate

This is a secondary analysis of de-identified, publicly available data from the Longitudinal Ageing Study in India (LASI) Wave 1. LASI obtained ethical approval from the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) and written informed consent from all participants. No additional ethics approval was required for this analysis.

## Consent for publication

Not applicable. The manuscript contains no individually identifiable data.

## Availability of data and material

LASI Wave 1 data are available to bona fide researchers on application through the Gateway to Global Aging Data (g2aging.org) and the International Institute for Population Sciences (IIPS). Derived analysis code, including the multi-reference spirometry classification, is available from the author on reasonable request.
