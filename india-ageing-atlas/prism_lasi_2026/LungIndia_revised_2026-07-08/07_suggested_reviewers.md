# Suggested Reviewers

**Manuscript:** How Much Preserved Ratio Impaired Spirometry in Older Indians Is Real? (LASI Wave 1).

The following independent areas of expertise are suggested for review. The author has no recent collaboration, shared institution, or conflict of interest with reviewers in these areas.

## Suggested reviewers

1. **Spirometry reference equations and lung-function measurement** — central to this manuscript, which compares five reference sets (GLI-2012, GLI-Global 2022 race-neutral, Chhabra-2014, Agarwal-2020, and an internal reference) and argues for nationally representative Indian equations. (Suggested area: pulmonary function / respiratory physiology.)

2. **Indian respiratory epidemiology / burden-of-disease estimation** — for the national prevalence framing and its public-health interpretation. (Suggested area: chest medicine / pulmonary epidemiology, India.)

3. **Ageing and frailty research using large population surveys (e.g., LASI, SAGE)** — for the analysis linking the spirometric patterns to frailty, handgrip strength, and functional limitation. (Suggested area: geriatric epidemiology.)

4. **Non-communicable disease and the nutrition transition in India** — for the metabolic-nutritional determinant profile (underweight, obesity, diabetes) that distinguishes PRISm from obstruction. (Suggested area: community medicine / NCD epidemiology.)

*Note to the Editor:* Named candidates with affiliations and e-mail addresses can be provided if preferred; generic descriptors are given here to avoid any appearance of suggesting close associates. The author requests that potential reviewers from his own institution be excluded.
