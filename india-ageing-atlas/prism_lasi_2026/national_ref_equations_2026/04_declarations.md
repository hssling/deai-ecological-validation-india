# Declarations

**Manuscript:** Nationally Representative Spirometry Reference Equations for Middle-Aged and Older Indians: Derivation and Validation from the Longitudinal Ageing Study in India, and the Re-estimated Burden of Restrictive and Preserved-Ratio Impairment.

**Author:** Dr Siddalingaiah H S, MD, Professor, Department of Community Medicine, Shridevi Institute of Medical Sciences and Research Hospital, Tumkur, Karnataka, India.

## Ethics approval and consent to participate

Secondary analysis of de-identified, publicly available data from the Longitudinal Ageing Study in India (LASI) Wave 1. LASI obtained ethical approval from the Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) and written informed consent from all participants. No additional approval was required.

## Consent for publication

Not applicable. The manuscript contains no individually identifiable data.

## Availability of data and material

LASI Wave 1 data are available to bona fide researchers through the Gateway to Global Aging Data (g2aging.org) and IIPS. The full derivation and validation code, the complete coefficient tables, and the lookup grid are available from the author on reasonable request.
