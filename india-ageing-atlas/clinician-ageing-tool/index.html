From patient data to focused next steps

Age, sex, height, spirometry, symptoms, exposure, and ageing-screen signals.

02 Score against LASI

FEV1, FVC, ratio, z-scores, LLN, percent predicted, and pattern flags.

Source and acknowledgements

Built on LASI national GAMLSS/LMS equations

The respiratory module reads the bundled LASI LMS table for FEV1, FVC, and FEV1/FVC, interpolates non-integer ages, applies height scaling for lung volumes, and computes z-scores, lower limits of normal, and percent predicted values.

LASI acknowledgement: this tool uses reference equations derived from public-use Longitudinal Ageing Study in India Wave 1 data, conducted by the International Institute for Population Sciences with the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health and the University of Southern California, supported by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India. We thank the LASI investigators and participants.
