Source and acknowledgements

Built on LASI national GAMLSS/LMS equations

The respiratory module reads the bundled LASI LMS table for FEV1, FVC, and FEV1/FVC, interpolates non-integer ages, applies height scaling for lung volumes, and computes z-scores, lower limits of normal, and percent predicted values.

LASI acknowledgement: this tool uses reference equations derived from public-use Longitudinal Ageing Study in India Wave 1 data, conducted by the International Institute for Population Sciences with the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health and the University of Southern California, supported by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India. We thank the LASI investigators and participants.

Concept, design, and creation: Dr Siddalingaiah H S, MD, Professor, Department of Community Medicine, Shridevi Institute of Medical Sciences and Research Hospital, Tumkur, Karnataka, India. ORCID: 0000-0002-4771-8285.

Intended age range 45-90 years; younger adults are not scored.
Current validation Internally validated on held-out LASI data; external clinical validation remains needed.
Patient privacy Client-side calculation only; no database or analytics endpoint is used.
Healthy ageing framework Designed around intrinsic capacity, functional ability, environment, person-centred goals, and follow-up.
Evidence backbone LASI respiratory equations, WHO ICOPE/Healthy Ageing concepts, WHO physical activity guidance, and falls-prevention logic.
Module maturity Education and prompts are active; diagnostic, prescribing, risk-prediction, and automated triage modules require separate validation.
Browser-only computation No patient identifiers, accounts, cookies, databases, or analytics endpoints are required.
Equation provenance The bundled LMS table is tested against the known reference-package examples before deployment.
